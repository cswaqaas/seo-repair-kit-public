## Spam Monitor Status

Spam Monitor is launched as a paid module. It helps customers review indexed Google results, detect suspicious URLs, configure Spam Rules, schedule monitoring, review alert history, and connect supported SERP providers when the module is active.

Free sites may use the SEO Repair Kit trial provider where available. The paid Spam Monitor module unlocks the full workflow and supported custom SERP provider connections.

## Choosing a Plan

- Individual: one website.
- Freelancer: up to 10 websites.
- Studio: up to 25 websites.
- Agency: up to 100 websites.
- Enterprise: up to 300 websites and larger usage needs.

Customers can manage active plans, connected websites, and license keys from `https://seorepairkit.com/my-account/`.
